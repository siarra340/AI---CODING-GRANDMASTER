About Us

🍅 Welcome to Susan: Your Go-to Dining Destination

Welcome to Susan, a lively neighborhood bistro where great food, loud laughter, and family traditions come together. Built on the idea that the best memories are made around a crowded table, our restaurant offers a relaxed, casual dining experience where everyone is treated like family from the moment they walk through the door.


Our menu is a celebration of simple, comforting classics done right, focusing on rustic, stone-baked pizzas and comforting, lovely pastas. While our pizzas feature beautifully charred crusts and premium toppings, the true heart of our kitchen lies in our pasta station.


We are incredibly proud to showcase our signature dish: Susan's Fresh Tomato Pasta. This crowd favorite features house-made, tender pasta tossed with vine-ripened tomatoes, fresh garlic, fragrant basil, and a generous drizzle of premium olive oil. It is a simple, vibrant dish that captures the exact essence of our cooking—fresh, honest, and full of love.
